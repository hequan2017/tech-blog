**NFS 生产环境  内核优化**

**cat >> /etc/sysctl.conf <<EOF**

**net.core.wmem_default = 8388608              ｗ发送**

**net.core.rmem_default = 8388608               r接收**

**net.core.rmem_max = 16777216**

**net.core.wmem_max = 1677216**

**EOF**
