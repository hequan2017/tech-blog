> **About this post**
>
> This post announces two cloud computing/OpenStack events held in Beijing in July 2016: the first is the "Cloud Computing Open Source Industry Alliance Achievements Release Conference" on July 13, held in the lecture hall on the second floor of the 3G Building of the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (registration via WeChat); the second is the 2016 China Cloud Computing User Conference (CCUC2016) on July 18 at the Beijing National Convention Center — simply fill in the registration form in the invitation attachment and reply by email. The phone number and email address of the registration contact, Chen Anyan, are listed at the end.

> **Technical notes**
>
> This post is 2016 event news; both meetings ended long ago. The registration phone number and email address are kept only as a historical record and may no longer be valid. For current OpenStack developments in China, look at the OpenInfra Foundation Summit and various cloud-native community events.
