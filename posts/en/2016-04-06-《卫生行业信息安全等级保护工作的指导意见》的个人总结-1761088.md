> **About this post**
>
> This post is my personal study summary of the Guiding Opinions on Classified Protection of Cybersecurity Work in the Health Industry (《卫生行业信息安全等级保护工作的指导意见》) issued by the Ministry of Health: the goal of the work is to carry out classified protection of cybersecurity (MLPS) grading and record-filing, construction and rectification, and classified protection evaluation in the health industry; the working principles are to follow standards and give priority protection, to combine industry guidance with territorial management, and to build in sync while improving dynamically; it also sorts out the division of the five security protection levels (the core business information systems of Grade-A tertiary hospitals are classified as Level 3, the supervised protection level; Level 3 and above require expert deliberation and annual evaluation), the standards on which construction and rectification are based, as well as publicity and training, supervision and inspection, and work requirements.

> **Technical notes**
>
> The guiding opinions were issued by the former Ministry of Health around 2011; the classified protection standards cited here, such as GB/T 22240-2008 and GB/T 22239-2008, have been superseded by the 2019 editions of the MLPS 2.0 framework (GB/T 22239-2019 and others), so grading, record-filing, evaluation, and rectification should now follow the new standards; the position that "core business systems of Grade-A tertiary hospitals are rated at Level 3" is still common today.

---

## 1. Work Objectives

In accordance with the regulations and following the specifications, carry out MLPS **grading and record-filing**, **construction and rectification**, and **classified protection evaluation** in the health industry.

## 2. Work Principles

- Follow **standards** and give **priority** protection
- Industry **guidance** and **territorial** management
- Synchronized **construction** and **dynamic** improvement (carry out MLPS concurrently)

## 3. Work Mechanisms

- Informatization Leading Group: the Ministry of Health
- Establish an information security technology **expert committee**

## 4. Work Tasks

### 4.1 Grading and Record-Filing

In accordance with Information Security Technology — Classification Guide for Classified Protection of Information Systems (《信息安全技术 信息系统安全等级保护定级指南》), GB22240-2008:

- Level 1: self-protection level
- Level 2: guided protection level
- **Level 3: supervised protection level — the core business information systems of Grade-A tertiary hospitals. Once the information system is damaged, it will cause serious damage to social order and the public interest, or damage to national security**
- Level 4: mandatory protection level
- Level 5: specialized control protection level

Level 3 and above must be deliberated and reviewed by the information security technology expert committee and filed with the public security authority of the locality.

### 4.2 Construction and Rectification

Based on:

- Information Security Technology — Baseline for Classified Protection of Information Systems (《信息安全技术 信息系统安全等级保护基本要求》), GB22239-2008
- Technical Requirements for Security Design of Classified Protection of Information Systems (《信息系统等级保护安全设计技术要求》)

The rectification plan must be deliberated by the expert committee: **improve facilities, establish rules and institutions, implement measures, and form a protection management system**.

### 4.3 Classified Protection Evaluation

In accordance with the Measures for the Administration of Classified Protection of Information Security (《信息安全等级保护管理办法》), the evaluation is performed by an evaluation institution and filed with the local authority, **once a year**.

### 4.4 Publicity and Training

- Training on MLPS policies, standards, and specifications
- Internal information security training

## 5. Supervision and Inspection

- The Informatization Leading Group is responsible for supervising and inspecting the implementation of MLPS by medical and health institutions across regions, and urging important information systems to carry out MLPS work
- Provincial health administrative departments are responsible for supervising the units in their own region
- Provincial departments shall report the progress of the work in their region to the Informatization Leading Group every year

## 6. Work Requirements

- Attach great importance and strengthen leadership
- Guarantee funding and strengthen oversight
- Strengthen communication and cooperate closely
