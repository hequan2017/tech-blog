> **About this post**
>
> Solves the problem where a GORM soft-delete model filters `deleted_at` by default, so deleted records cannot be queried: explicitly append `deleted_at is null` to the `Where` condition and call `Unscoped()` to skip GORM's default soft-delete scope, achieving exact matching of non-deleted data.

> **Technical notes**
>
> The example is written against GORM v1; in GORM v2 the soft-delete field type changed to `gorm.DeletedAt`, and `Unscoped()` behaves the same but requires importing the `gorm.io/gorm` package. The content remains generally applicable today; just mind SQL casing and database compatibility.

---

```go
err = db.Where("name = ?", name).Where("deleted_at is null").Unscoped().First(&a).Error
```
