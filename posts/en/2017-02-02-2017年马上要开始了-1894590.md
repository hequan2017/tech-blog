Tomorrow is the first working day of the year. My goal for the next three months is to go over all the Linux knowledge I learned before, one more time.

This time last year, I started teaching myself Linux. I never mastered it—I just went through it roughly once, which at least counts as getting started.

From February through April, before the May Day holiday, I will reorganize everything I know.

And write it up as proper technical documentation.

The goal is to eventually produce a book on the fundamentals—purely pragmatic in spirit.
