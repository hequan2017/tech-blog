> **About this post**
>
> This post shares a Zabbix 3.4 template for monitoring the hardware of
> DELL servers, `Template Dell iDrac SNMPV2.xml`. It collects hardware
> information such as CPU, voltage, temperature, and fans via the iDRAC
> hardware management card over SNMP V2. The author localized the
> original template into Chinese, so all items and triggers are
> displayed in Chinese, and it can be imported into Zabbix and used
> directly. The template is hosted in the GitHub repository
> zabbix-models; a screenshot of the item list after import is
> attached at the end.

> **Technical notes**
>
> 1. Zabbix 2.x/3.x are no longer maintained; upgrading to Zabbix 6.0 LTS or 7.0 LTS is recommended.
> 2. The official template library of Zabbix 6.0+ already includes the "DELL PowerEdge by SNMP" template family. New deployments should prefer the official templates instead of importing this third-party Chinese-localized version.
> 3. The repository link in the original post lost its domain name; it has been restored as github.com.
