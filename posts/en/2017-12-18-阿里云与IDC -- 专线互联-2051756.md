> **About this post**
>
> This post documents the hands-on process of interconnecting an Alibaba Cloud
> VPC with a self-built IDC data center over a physical dedicated line: first
> draw the topology and contact the provider to activate the line, then view
> the Virtual Border Router (VBR) in the Express Connect console and create
> router interfaces using the prepaid "Dedicated Line Access" option (the
> region must match the border router's), and finally add a route entry for
> the peer network segment on the VPC router pointing to the router interface,
> enabling mutual access between the two internal networks.

> **Technical notes**
>
> The product form has evolved several times: the VBR + router interface model
> remains in use today, but for cross-account/cross-region networking Alibaba
> Cloud now recommends CEN (Cloud Enterprise Network) with automatic route
> learning, removing the need for manual bidirectional configuration; a
> physical dedicated line requires construction work by a carrier, and access
> points, billing, and SLA are subject to the current official documentation.
