> **About this post**
>
> This is a short celebratory post: in November 2017 the author successfully
> passed the Alibaba Cloud Professional (ACP) certification exam in Cloud
> Computing. The post includes a screenshot of the official certificate issued
> by Alibaba Cloud (holder: Quan He; certificate number ACP01171100148194;
> valid for two years, until November 10, 2019). It does not cover specific
> exam questions or preparation details.

> **Technical notes**
>
> 1. The Alibaba Cloud ACP certificate is valid for 2 years from the date of
>    issue; upon expiry, the exam must be retaken to renew it.
> 2. Alibaba Cloud's certification system has undergone several adjustments.
>    Currently, ACP corresponds to the Cloud Computing track within the
>    "Alibaba Cloud ACE/ACP Professional" series; the exam syllabus (ECS, SLB,
>    OSS, VPC, security groups, Auto Scaling, etc.) is subject to the latest
>    official version.
