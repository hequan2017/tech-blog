> **About this post**
>
> This post documents how to build a CentOS 7 automated installation USB drive: modify the isolinux.cfg boot menu, add `ks=cdrom:/isolinux/ks.cfg` pointing to the kickstart file, and provide a complete ks.cfg example covering partitioning, the root password, timezone, packages, and other settings, to achieve an unattended installation.
>
> **Technical notes**
>
> CentOS 7 reached end of life (EOL) on June 30, 2024; consider Rocky Linux 9 / AlmaLinux 9 or openEuler 22.03 LTS instead. MD5 password hashes generated by `openssl passwd -1` are no longer secure; modern systems should use `openssl passwd -6` (sha512).

## How to Build the USB Drive

Download a CentOS 7 image, delete `isolinux/isolinux.cfg` inside it, and save the two files above under isolinux/.

Re-create the USB drive, and it will install the system automatically. There are 2 options in total; the one below is the manual install.
