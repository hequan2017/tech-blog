> **About this post**
>
> A QR code printed in the terminal of a Huawei server (PCDN scenario) came out garbled.
> Switching to the GoTerminalQRCode project, packaging it as a binary, and running it
> with the `-t` flag makes it display correctly.

> **Technical notes**
>
> GoTerminalQRCode is a small Go utility that generates QR codes in the terminal (forked
> from LGiki/GoTerminalQRCode); `-t` enables truecolor (24-bit color) mode, which requires
> a truecolor-capable terminal. Common causes of garbled terminal QR codes: the terminal
> encoding is not UTF-8, the font lacks block element characters, or the terminal does not
> support truecolor.
